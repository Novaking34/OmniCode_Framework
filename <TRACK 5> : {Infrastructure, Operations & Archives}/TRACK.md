# <TRACK 5> : {Infrastructure, Operations & Archives}

## Scope & Invariant

Operations, containerization, DNS synchronization, packaging, and daemon management.

- `infra/dist/`: Standalone distribution packages (`.tar.gz`).
- `infra/dns/`: Dynamic DNS synchronization scripts & zone records.
- `infra/docker/`: Multi-architecture containerization and composition topologies.
- `infra/scripts/`: Toolchain packaging, cloud synchronization, and deployment automation.
- `infra/systemd/`: Production daemon service definitions.
